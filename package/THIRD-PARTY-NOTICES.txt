Gen5W Auto DST is a standalone NEMESIS port of Cabinwright Tweaks auto-dst.
Original author: hazptr / Cabinwright.
Upstream commit: c593aa1dc115f2596554e94f9a5363052bb29202
https://github.com/cabinwright/tweaks/tree/c593aa1dc115f2596554e94f9a5363052bb29202/package/tweaks/auto-dst

bin/auto-dst.sh retains the upstream MIT license. Changes: explicit copyright/
license headers and a Gen5W-specific status filename to avoid log collisions.
